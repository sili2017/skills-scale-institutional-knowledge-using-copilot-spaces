# README: OctoAcme Project Management Processes Summary & Links

This README is the entry point to OctoAcme’s project management documentation. The process starts with **initiation**, where teams validate the problem, define measurable outcomes, align stakeholders, and confirm a go/no-go decision using a one-pager, initial timeline, and early risk list. In **planning**, approved work is translated into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, dependencies, and a shared Definition of Done.

During **execution and tracking**, teams deliver in small increments through a visible board workflow (Backlog → Ready → In Progress → In Review → QA → Done), regular standups, weekly syncs, and sprint or milestone demos. Pull requests are expected to stay small, link back to issues, and pass CI checks before review to keep delivery predictable and transparent.

In **release and deployment**, OctoAcme uses pre-release quality gates (acceptance criteria complete, CI/security checks passing, release notes, rollback planning), staged deployment, post-deploy verification, and stakeholder announcements. The lifecycle closes with **retrospectives and continuous improvement**, where teams capture lessons learned, assign owners to action items, and feed improvements back into planning and execution.

Across all phases, delivery relies on clear role ownership (Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, QA/Testing, and Stakeholders), consistent communication cadence, active risk management through a maintained risk register and escalation paths, and layered quality assurance (unit, integration, smoke, security, and manual QA as needed).
